# Whittaker House Environment

A horror house environment built around a three-floor Victorian mansion with dark atmosphere, unsettling details, and multiple rooms per floor.

### Basement
- Basement Landing

- Cellar
- Laundry Room
- Hidden Room

## Design Notes

- Top Floor: narrow, claustrophobic, and eerie. Use low ceilings, flickering lights, and objects that feel out of place.
- Ground Floor: main narrative space with branching exploration. The foyer connects to the living room, dining room, kitchen, and stairs.
- Basement: darkest and most oppressive. The hidden room is a strong horror beat with secret story details.

## Suggested Gameplay Hooks

- Place a key or clue in the Guest Bedroom that unlocks the Hidden Room in the basement.
- Use the Dining Room and Kitchen to create a decayed, abandoned meal scene.
- Add environmental sounds in the Boiler Room and Attic Loft to build tension.
- Make the broken mirror in the Top Bathroom and the scratched messages in the Hidden Room important narrative elements.

## Implementation Ideas

- If you use a game engine, import this layout and connect room transitions via doors and staircases.
- Add ambient audio layers per floor: wind and rats in the attic, old piano and whispers on the ground floor, dripping water and distant machinery in the basement.
- Use the JSON structure for room metadata, descriptions, and connection mapping.
